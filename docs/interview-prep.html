30-Second Pitch

I built an ML pipeline that processes 3.8 million French property transactions from government open data, cleans them to 997,000 quality records, and produces price estimates for 674,000 geographic zones—from country down to building parcels. Model achieves R²=0.517 with hierarchical smoothing for sparse data.

🧠 Smoothing Formula (Draw this!)

weight = min(1.0, n / 50)
smoothed = weight × local + (1 - weight) × parent

Example: Commune 10 tx at €2,000, dept avg €1,500:
weight = 10/50 = 0.2
smoothed = 0.2×€2,000 + 0.8×€1,500 = €1,600/m²
